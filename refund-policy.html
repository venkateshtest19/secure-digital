Refund Policy

Clear and transparent terms for your digital software purchases.

At Secure Digital, we strive to ensure complete customer satisfaction. However, because we sell digital software license keys that are delivered instantly, our refund policy is strictly defined to protect both our business and our customers. By purchasing from Secure Digital, you agree to the following terms.

1. General Policy for Digital Goods

Due to the nature of digital products, all sales are considered final once the license key has been generated, revealed, or delivered to the customer via email, SMS, or phone. Once a key is provided, it is considered "consumed" and is strictly non-refundable, non-exchangeable, and non-transferable.

2. Valid Refund Claims

We stand behind the authenticity of our products. We will issue a full refund or a free replacement ONLY under the following specific conditions:

  • Invalid or Defective Key: The provided license key is expired, already in use, or fails to activate, and our support team is unable to resolve the issue with the manufacturer.
  • Incorrect Product Delivered: We accidentally sent you a license key for a different product or a different duration than what you purchased and paid for.
  • Non-Delivery: You have not received your license key within a reasonable timeframe after payment confirmation, and we are unable to resolve the delivery issue.

3. Invalid Refund Claims

Refunds will NOT be granted for the following reasons:

  • You changed your mind after the key was delivered.
  • You purchased the wrong product by mistake (please verify your device compatibility and needs with our sales team before purchasing).
  • You are experiencing technical issues with the software itself (please contact the software manufacturer's official support, as your genuine key makes you eligible for their free technical assistance).
  • You violated the software manufacturer's End User License Agreement (EULA), resulting in the key being blocked or revoked.

4. How to Request a Refund or Replacement

If you believe your purchase qualifies for a refund or replacement, you must adhere to the following process:

  1. Timeframe: You must contact us within 24 hours of receiving your license key.
  2. Contact Method: Call us at +1-855-301-0308 or email us at securedigital@atomicmail.io.
  3. Required Information: Please provide your full name, the email/phone number used for the purchase, the product name, and clear proof of the activation error (e.g., a screenshot of the error message).

Our team will investigate the issue promptly. If the claim is validated, we will process a replacement key immediately or issue a refund to the original payment method within 3-5 business days.

5. Order Cancellations

If you wish to cancel your order, you must contact us before the license key has been generated and delivered. Once the key has been sent, the order cannot be canceled, and the General Policy for Digital Goods applies.
